# Shared quote ingress: current price line

The previous native price marker still consumed pane output. Its futures route required the active-page/priority dispatch, pane identity and outlier validation, and completion of synchronous paper-order / mark callbacks. The watchlist published before those gates. Passing a test that injected directly into the pane marker did not test that difference.

This repair publishes an instrument/broker/contract-scoped chart quote at the watchlist ingress, before those pane paths. Shared Chart subscribes to that channel plus its existing pane execution fallback. Repeated observations across both channels are deduplicated, older observations cannot overwrite newer ones, replay unsubscribes, and disposal removes both listeners. Quotes do not modify OHLC, execution history or studies. No extra network requests or provider subscriptions.

Read-only VPS sample, 9 November: 55 changing NQ quotes in 10 seconds; median gap 198 ms, p95 273 ms, max 333 ms. Separate execution channel: 33 batches, median gap 88 ms, p95 1071 ms, max 1684 ms. A second 12-second simultaneous ordering probe observed 70 quote packets and zero quotes rejected by the cross-channel timestamp check. Thus timestamp suppression was investigated but was NOT established as the cause. Its first retained quote was 64 seconds old; subsequent examples were 13–65 ms old. Neither sample establishes multi-hour market-open reliability.

18 focused tests pass, including extracting the actual watchlist publisher and verifying chart delivery happens before watchlist scheduling, without invoking a pane or order callback. Contract, broker, symbol, stale observations, fallback deduplication and cleanup are checked.

Actual shared Chart + Lightweight Charts headless Chrome: 180 shared-ingress quotes over NQ 1m, ES 5m, GC 1s, SPY 1m, QQQ 200v and NDX 10r. p95 synchronous ingress-to-native-series call 0.1 ms; maximum 0.4 ms. Canvas pixels change; no candle-history writes during quote injection; replay isolation and live return pass; no browser errors. These timings are not exchange latency or full-workspace stress measurements. Fixture candles are deterministic. Existing standalone GEX Data / liquidity-map engines are not rewritten.

Production build and deployment recorded after verification. No live gateway/terminal/subscription restart, owner-tab refresh, order/account/workspace mutation or spending increase. This removes a concrete chart/watchlist delivery-path discrepancy; it does not prove that every upstream pause or overloaded-main-thread stall is eliminated.
