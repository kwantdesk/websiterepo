# Book Speed overhaul

Twelfth in the chart indicator checklist. The study measures how many displayed bid and ask price levels are consumed within a seconds window or tick reversal. DeepChart documents both modes, bid and ask histogram colours and width, moving averages and threshold markers. KwantDesk also exposes pane height and retained history. [DeepChart Book Speed](https://www.deepcharts.com/helpcenter/deepdom/article/book-speed)

## Findings and changes

- A single matching print could make a disappearing 100-contract level count as fully consumed. Book Speed now requires enough aggressive quantity at the same price and side to cover the previously displayed level. It ignores older book frames and rejects comparisons across an invalid book, a disconnect or skipped measurement windows. A stale fixture used to produce 17 synthetic completed zero buckets. It now produces zero.
- Presentation quotes could advance the book clock or drive a tick reversal. The shared adapter now preserves authoritative depth timestamp, validity and last trade price separately and flags quote-only frames. Book Speed measures depth frames and calculates reversals in whole ticks.
- Micros could silently use a parent book. Book Speed now scopes to the exact chart root and validated contract. The isolated chart requested MNQ even with stale NQ contract metadata and ignored a subsequent NQ frame. Switching the chart interval reused the same depth stream.
- Book measurements could fall between the native chart's time coordinates. The pane now maps windows to host candles and sums their counts when multiple windows occupy one candle. The tooltip reports how many windows contributed and distinguishes candle totals from the rolling per-window average. Window times follow the selected chart timezone.
- The marker toggle now matches its default-off state. The dialog explains when Average and Marker settings are disabled. Average length and history visibility can update from retained observations. A rolling sum computes averages at the pane's publish cadence, without rebuilding the whole history for each depth message.

## Verification and remaining checks

- Focused engine and leased-stream tests passed. They cover positive consumption, insufficient executions, out-of-order and invalid book frames, tick-only messages, seconds and reversal modes, rollover, exact micros, time and event-bar placement, and bounded history. The broad replay suite reported five inventory and source-assertion failures outside these focused checks. They remain visible in the saved test log.
- The isolated chart rendered two consumed ask levels on one host candle, ignored a wrong-contract frame and presentation tick, kept the subscription on an interval change, closed it in replay, and reported no browser errors. The settings check exercised average and marker visibility, the marker value, all six custom colours, histogram width, pane height and persistence. The settings dialog measured 358 px in a 390 px viewport.
- A read-only VPS snapshot verified valid exact MNQZ6, ESZ6 and MESZ6 books. The NQ root response was a transitional invalid book. A separate 5.5-second MNQZ6 stream returned 31 matching valid depth frames. Its observed exchange timestamp spacing was 149 ms median and 320 ms at the 95th percentile. Local and source clocks differed, so this does not establish delivery latency or cadence for other contracts.
- The owned archive stores depth and executions. The website's existing replay packs sample depth at two seconds and cap retained trades at 256 per frame. They can miss transient exhaustion or reversals and cannot establish equivalent Book Speed measurements. A real NQ pack probe returned a pending build. Book Speed stays unavailable in replay until an exact recorded measurement source exists. The current live chart uses retained stream frames where supplied, with its actual lookback bounded by available observations.
- Static REA found no Book Speed member in the 87 retained methods of DeepChart's indicator-description type. The DLL metadata is partial and this check cannot determine the implementation. Keep desktop comparison open for identical contracts, window boundaries, reversal timing, level counts, averages, controls, and historical coverage. The live engine still measures the sampled book it receives and can miss levels depleted and replenished between snapshots.
